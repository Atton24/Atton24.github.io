Food Fight
Unity • WebGL
A two‑player arena game where each player fires food‑themed projectiles while dodging spinning obstacles. Created as part of CS 328 – Game Design. Credits: Daniel Thomas.

FoxyBoys
Unity • WebGL
A cooperative puzzle‑platformer where players switch between two fox characters — one who builds blocks and one who destroys them — to solve levels. Created by Caleb Gesick, Jared Manning, Daniel Thomas, and Christine Vaughan.

ShapeQuest
Unity • WebGL
A precision movement game where the hero follows the mouse cursor to collect coins, avoid hazards, and reach the exit. Created by Caleb Gesick, Jared Manning, Daniel Thomas, and Christine Vaughan.
