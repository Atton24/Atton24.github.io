Food Fight

A Unity WebGL game built by Daniel Thomas.

Food Fight Created by: Daniel Thomas

How to Play

🎯 Objective
Hit your opponent 20 times to win. Use spinning walls and obstacles to dodge attacks and create openings.
🕹️ Player 1 Controls (Right)
• Move: Arrow Keys
• Shoot: Right CTRL
🕹️ Player 2 Controls (Left)
• Move: WASD
• Shoot: F
📌 Notes
• Local multiplayer only
• Not mobile friendly
• Created by Daniel Thomas for CS 328 – Game Design
