About

A systems‑minded generalist with a bias toward automation, observability, and making complex stacks feel boring—in the best way.

I lead IT systems and operations at Luxer One, where I own infrastructure, networking, identity, and endpoint systems for 350+ employees and 500+ devices.

I engineered zero‑touch provisioning and automated device onboarding that improved efficiency by 99% and eliminated manual setup. I’ve led MDM migrations (Meraki → Jamf → Intune), strengthened security with password policies, endpoint encryption, and app approval workflows, and driven multisite network upgrades from UniFi to Meraki/Cisco.

I also build user lifecycle automation using SAML 2.0, APIs, and webhooks to reduce manual HRIS/SSO/ITSM work, and I manage SSO integrations for tools like Jamf, Meraki SM, Rhombus, Openpath, Slack, Zoom, NICE InContact, Yodeck, and Jira.

I studied Computer Science and Engineering with a Mathematics minor at the University of Nevada, Reno. That background shows up in how I think about systems: clear models, good defaults, and reliable behavior over time.
