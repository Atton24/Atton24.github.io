About

A systems‑minded generalist with a bias toward automation, observability, and making complex stacks feel boring—in the best way.

I lead IT systems and operations at Luxer One, where I’m responsible for the infrastructure that powers smart locker networks and internal tooling. My work spans Windows and Linux servers, virtualization, networking, identity, and SaaS integrations.

I care about reliability, clarity, and good defaults. That means building guardrails, not gates; automating the repetitive parts; and giving teams visibility into what’s happening without drowning them in alerts.

Before that, I studied Computer Science (with a Math minor) at the University of Nevada, Reno. These days, you’ll find me tuning infrastructure, refining monitoring, or experimenting with clean, wave‑inspired visual systems for the tools I build.
